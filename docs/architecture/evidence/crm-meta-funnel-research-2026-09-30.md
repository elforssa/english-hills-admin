## What the sources do and do not establish

**Recommended sending:** a genuine initial/raw CRM receipt plus subsequent commercial-stage changes. An Intake event for every eligible, accepted new opportunity addresses coverage directly. Sending repeated Qualified events for the same lead does not increase distinct-lead coverage. Sending negatives can describe unsuccessful outcomes, consistent with comprehensive update guidance, but no inspected source individually mandates the exact names `Lost` or `Not qualified`, promises a negative-label learning mechanism, or quantifies their incremental advertising benefit.

**Validation:** one valid event establishes connection, not optimization readiness. Stage-count, volume, coverage and observation-period requirements are separate. F3's 60% threshold is a minimum; F5's example expects matching uploads for all generated leads. Neither permits weakening D2 to reach a metric. An HTTP receipt count alone establishes none of these cohort-level requirements.

**Positive versus negative:** propose Intake as entry/receipt, Qualified and Converted as positive outcomes, Lost and Not qualified as non-positive closures. Do not order negative closures after Converted as if they were successes. In Events Manager, follow F4 by excluding negatives from the positive sales funnel. Exact current account controls and whether Intake is retained as entry/other need an authorized read-only configuration manifest before a later operator change.

**Optimization:** Qualified is an English Hills recommendation, not a Meta-prescribed name. It is the earliest explicit business-quality decision, with Converted as the later trusted outcome. Intake is volume/coverage, not quality; attempted contact is effort, not quality. Engaged records a conversation but has not independently established suitability. Qualified's actual volume, delay and conversion rate are unknown; assess them before selecting the target. Meta may optimize downstream/differently.

**B versus C:** B is a minimized commercial funnel, a deliberate interpretation of advertiser-defined stages rather than literal export of every EH operational state. Meta's broad all-stage guidance leaves an applicability risk when omitting Contacting/Engaged. Public evidence does not prove those two extra disclosures improve performance, and C offers no additional distinct-lead coverage when Intake succeeds. Keep omission visible for owner approval and account validation; if Meta requires them for this integration, stop for a separately approved C revision.

## Revision 4 ordering interpretation and owner direction

On 2026-09-30 the owner approved B in principle, activity-backed repeat occurrences and the initial Qualified target, and directed chronological attempt ordering before final architecture approval. F1/F2 motivate sending actual earlier stages; the inspected evidence does not establish confirmed receipt of every predecessor as a prerequisite for a later genuine event. The revised plan holds an uncertain identity against replay while permitting later independently eligible outcomes after the earlier send boundary, preserving its unknown audit trail. This is an explicit architecture tradeoff for useful lifecycle coverage, not a newly verified Meta guarantee. No public sources were re-accessed for this owner-direction update. If applicable provider evidence establishes a confirmed-receipt requirement, stop and revise the architecture rather than silently change ordering or replay policy.
