# Owner summary

**Enrollment at payment.** Today an enrollment is born as a « pré-inscription » (Submitted, Under Review) or an « essai » (Trial), waits for a payment, and a reception payment confirms it only from 10 % of the net price; below that the director has to confirm. From this release, **an enrollment exists only once it is paid**: reception enrolls and collects in one step, any payment above zero confirms, and the remaining balance stays tracked as today (« Acompte versé », « En retard »). The three waiting statuses and the « Pré-inscriptions » page are retired, never dropped: old rows stay readable in the learner file, and a database guard prevents any new one.

## What will change

- **One flow, « Inscrire et encaisser »**, for walk-ins (from the learner file or the receipt form) and for CRM prospects (from the prospect panel): programme, school year, level, group (optional), price, discount with its reason, payment. One click creates the confirmed enrollment and the receipt together; a chosen group is assigned right after (a full group never blocks the payment: the learner is then « Inscrit — groupe à affecter »).
- **Any payment above zero confirms** (owner decision 2 withdraws Batch E Q5 = B). The message « Acompte inférieur à 10 % … » and the director list « Acomptes inférieurs à 10 % en attente de confirmation » disappear.
- **A CRM prospect becomes « Converti » at that moment** and its « Finaliser l'inscription » task closes, through the existing trusted conversion rule (unchanged: only a linked Confirmed/Validated enrollment converts).
- **« Pré-inscription » and « Essai » disappear** from reception, the CRM dialog, the learner file, the enrollments page, the dashboard, the Students list filters, the public form, the parent portal and the guide. The « Pré-inscriptions » page is removed; « Clôturer l'année » moves (Q7).
- **The public self sign-up is turned off**: `/inscription` becomes a short « contact us » page (call, WhatsApp, visit) and `/api/public/inscription` refuses with 410. The website's « S'inscrire » buttons are changed in a separate small eh-website PR (listed below); its contact/inquiry form (`/api/public/crm-inquiry`) is untouched.
- **Existing data** (counts first, on a read-only Production query): unpaid pré-inscriptions and essais are **archived** (status « Terminée » with the reason « Pré-inscription abandonnée » or « Essai terminé »), never deleted; every pré-inscription or essai with a live payment becomes confirmed (Validated when it has a group), which converts its CRM prospect; prospects whose pré-inscription was archived are detached from it so they can be enrolled again (Q6).

## What staff/users will be able to do

- **Reception:** enroll and collect in one step for a walk-in or a prospect; give a discount with a reason as today (Batch H); assign the group at the same time or later. A fully discounted enrollment (100 %, with a reason) is confirmed without a receipt and listed in « Contrôles » (Q1, recommended). **Reception places a learner in a group only when a paid enrollment of that programme and year exists** (Q3 = B, proposed): the group page, the learner file, the learner form and « Groupe à affecter » all refuse otherwise with one message. A prospect whose learner already paid at the desk is linked to that paid enrollment without a second payment (Q12).
- **Director / admin:** the same flow; they may still place a learner without an enrollment, with a logged reason; « Contrôles » (director only) keeps its four sections, the 10 % section replaced by « Inscriptions confirmées sans aucun paiement » (Q3, Q11, Q13).
- **Nobody** can create a pré-inscription or an essai any more: reception, management, the public form, the parent portal and any direct write are refused by the database with one French message.

## What remains restricted

- Roles, RLS, the finance engine's numbering, discounts, dates and idempotency, the enrollment/group invariants of Batch D and H, and the conversion authority are unchanged. Reception still cannot void, correct, end or reopen an enrollment, nor exceed a group's capacity.
- Conversion still comes only from a trusted linked Confirmed/Validated enrollment; the new flow produces exactly that, in the same transaction as the receipt.
- No new permission is added. Two reception-reachable functions lose their pre-enrollment branch; one CRM function loses its direct API grant and is reached only through the new wrapper.

## UI impact

- Learner file: the button « Nouvelle pré-inscription » / « Nouvelle inscription » becomes **« Inscrire et encaisser »** (opens the receipt form for that learner). « Modifier l'inscription » keeps level, date and notes; status is read-only; the group goes through placement as today.
- Receipt form (`/receipts/new`): a « Groupe (facultatif) » field for tuition sessions; the 10 % notice disappears; a 100 % discount shows « Inscription confirmée sans paiement, visible dans Contrôles ».
- Prospect panel: « Commencer l'inscription » becomes **« Inscrire et encaisser »**, a three-step dialog (learner, enrollment, payment); « Continuer l'inscription » disappears (no waiting state); the success panel says « Inscription confirmée · reçu EH-… ».
- Menu: « Inscriptions » (the « Pré-inscriptions » page) and « Formulaire public » are removed. Dashboard: the « Pré-inscriptions en attente » card becomes « Inscrits — groupe à affecter ».
- Students list: the « Essai (dossier) » filter value goes; « Groupe à affecter » only for a Confirmed enrollment without group.
- Guide (`/helpdesk`): C6 rewritten as one step; Q3 and Q7 rewritten; status reference cards, the message table and « J'ai fait une erreur » updated; four screenshots retaken (s08, q02b, q07, q07b).
- Labels of historical rows stay readable: « Soumise », « En cours d'examen », « Essai », « Refusée » only appear on old rows and on ended ones (with their reason).
- Public: `/inscription` becomes a static contact page (no form, no Turnstile); the privacy page's pré-inscription paragraphs are reworded (historical consent rows keep their stored policy version string).

## Database impact

  - `create_charge_payment` re-created from its 122 body without the 10 % branch, with the free-enrollment branch (Q1) and the detach-aware link check;
  - `get_finance_review_queue` re-created from 122 with section (d) replaced (Q3);
  - `crm_start_enrollment` re-created from 125 to create only Confirmed enrollments, API grant revoked; new wrapper `crm_enroll_and_collect` (enrollment + payment in one transaction);
  - `save_receptionist_enrollment` (125), `create_enrollment` and `save_enrollment_changes` (120), `create_public_registration` (125) and `portal_request_reenrollment` (125) re-created so their pre-enrollment branches refuse;
  - `crm_security.protect_enrollment_link` (084) re-created to admit the archive step's detach under a transaction-local flag (Q6), the same exemption the approved [LIR-r1 step 2e](lead-intake-reliability.md) will reuse.
- **One separately approved, counted, journalled data step** (`supabase/manual/`), after the migration, in the same window: confirm the paid rows, archive the unpaid ones, detach the linked prospects, with a revert script from its journal (the Batch D clean-up pattern).
- **Nothing is dropped.** Dropping the three values from the constraint, the dead branches of readers that still list them, and the unused rate-limit scope is a later optional release (owner decision 6).

## Important security decisions

- **The payment function keeps the authority.** Confirmation still happens inside `create_charge_payment` (finance capability, stored role), in the same transaction as the receipt. The CRM wrapper composes the existing CRM command and the existing payment command; it adds no new authority and both inner commands keep their own idempotency.
- **A direct enrollment creation is closed for every role**, including `service_role` and the public form: the guard is a trigger, like the Batch D and H guards, so no RPC, policy or client can create a waiting enrollment.
- **The public endpoint is retired, not merely hidden**: the route answers 410 and the definer function it called refuses; the Turnstile secret, the website proxy secret and the inquiry endpoint are untouched.
- **The archive step converts prospects** (every paid pré-inscription becomes Confirmed, so its lead becomes CONVERTED through the existing trigger). This is the intended effect of owner decision 5 and is counted first (category B/C/E/G of the count query). **These conversions are permanent**: a converted lead's conversion fields are immutable (`protect_enrollment_link`, 084 lines 8–15), CRM activities are append-only (078 lines 270–278) and cancelled tasks are not restored; the revert restores enrollment rows only. The owner accepts this at R2 approval, on the day's counts. The lifecycle outbox stays dormant: no destination, no epoch, no delivery (verified at R4).
- **The CRM wrapper never commits an enrollment without its receipt** (review B1): a payment of 0 MAD or any payment the inner command accepts without issuing a receipt makes the wrapper raise, rolling back the enrollment, the lead link, the conversion and the task cancellations; the only exceptions are the 100 % discount (Q1 = A) and the link of an already paid enrollment (Q12).

## Risks / owner review points

1. **Q1 (free enrollment)** and **Q2 (enrollment without payment by management)** decide whether an enrollment can ever exist without a receipt. The recommendation keeps exactly one such path (100 % discount with a reason, listed in Contrôles).
2. **Q3 (proposed B for reception)**: placement from the group page today creates a Validated enrollment with no payment (Batch D, `academic_security.place` line 532); the plan closes that for reception on every path and keeps it for director/admin with a logged reason, listed in Contrôles.
3. **Q6**: prospects linked to an archived pré-inscription must be detached, or they can never be enrolled again from the CRM (the link is immutable by design, 084). Archiving an unpaid row linked to an already **converted** lead raises a conversion review flag (the existing downgrade rule); the count query gives the expected number (G7).
4. **Learner files of archived pré-inscriptions become « Inactif »** (Q4), which is what a refused pré-inscription already does today; the file is then outside the default Students list.
5. **The archive step changes statuses on Production rows and converts leads, and those conversions are irreversible** (immutable conversion fields, append-only activities, cancelled tasks not restored). The journal lets the revert restore the enrollment rows only; the owner accepts the conversions when approving R2 on the day's counts. Conversions made by the step carry the step's timestamp as `converted_at`, so the director funnel counts them in the release month, not in the month they were paid (G2 rows).
6. **Q11–Q13 (new, proposed)**: archived pré-inscriptions and essais can never be reopened; reopening a real ended enrollment stays director-only and shows in Contrôles; a prospect whose learner already paid is linked without a second payment; the unpaid Confirmed/Validated rows that already exist (Batch D « Rattrapage EGC » included) are counted at R0 and appear in Contrôles until handled.
7. **Rollout order is migration first, app within minutes, outside reception hours.** Between the two, the old application refuses pre-enrollment creation with the guard's French message, takes payments without the 10 % rule, and refuses a director/admin placement of a learner without an enrollment (the old build cannot send the new reason); nothing else breaks. A failed archive step changes nothing (one transaction) and stops the release before the deploy unless the owner explicitly accepts deploying with the waiting rows still in place.
8. **The website keeps pointing at `/inscription` until its PR is merged**; the page shows the contact information meanwhile, so no visitor meets an error.

# Design

## D1 — Status model and the database guard

After this release an enrollment is **Confirmed** (paid, awaiting a group), **Validated** (paid, in a group) or **Ended** (history with a reason). **Submitted**, **Under Review**, **Trial** and (Q5) **Rejected** are *retired*: valid in the constraint, displayed on old rows with their French label, never created or re-entered.

- New trigger **`enrollments_retired_status_guard`** (`before insert or update of status on public.enrollments for each row execute function academic_security.guard_retired_enrollment_status()`), for every role: raises `23514` with hint `eap.status_retired` and the message « La pré-inscription et l'essai n'existent plus : l'inscription se confirme à l'encaissement. » when `new.status` is a retired value and (insert, or `old.status` is distinct from `new.status`). Exemption only when both `eh.membership_sync` and `eh.enrollment_command` are `'on'` (the consumed Batch D revert and this plan's archive revert, exactly as 125's year guard). The archive step itself never needs it: it only moves rows *out* of retired values.
- Firing order: PostgreSQL fires same-event row triggers in alphabetical order of their names, so on `enrollments` the `before` triggers run as `enrollment_group_year_guard` (125), **`enrollment_student_sync_before`** (120, `check_enrollment_student_sync`: it rewrites Confirmed-with-group into Validated and refuses Validated/Trial without a group), `enrollments_history_guard` (120), then **`enrollments_retired_status_guard`**; the `after` triggers (`enrollment_student_sync_after`, `storage_enrollment_binding`, `zz_crm_enrollment_status`) follow. The order is immaterial to correctness: the sync trigger never writes a retired value, each guard refuses its own condition, and the rehearsal asserts that an ended row and a retired transition are each refused with their own hint.
- No constraint, column or index changes. The French label map (`ENROLLMENT_LABELS`, `ENROLLMENT_STATUS_COLORS`) keeps the four retired entries for history and gains nothing.

## D3 — One flow: « Inscrire et encaisser »

**Walk-in (receipt form, `/receipts/new`).** The form already creates the learner, the charge and the receipt, and the wrapper already confirms or creates the enrollment. Changes: (a) the learner file's button becomes « Inscrire et encaisser » → `/receipts/new?student_id=…`; (b) a « Groupe (facultatif) » select appears for tuition sessions once programme, year and level are chosen, listing groups of that programme/level/year with their free seats (read through the existing `crm_enrollment_groups` or the groups entity; the database remains the authority); (c) after a successful payment that returns `enrollment_id`, the page re-reads **both the learner row and the enrollment row** (`id, updated_at`) and calls `place_learner_in_group(student, group, student_updated_at, enrollment, enrollment_updated_at)` — the placement core requires the named enrollment's `updated_at` under `p_require_fresh` (120 lines 460–466, else `egc.stale`), and the payment has just bumped it (S5); a refusal (full group, year mismatch, stale) shows « Paiement enregistré. Groupe non affecté : <message>. Apprenant inscrit — groupe à affecter. » and never undoes the payment. Placement is a second command on purpose: a full group must not roll back a numbered receipt.

**Reception placement needs an existing enrollment (Q3 = B for reception, proposed).** `academic_security.place` (120 line 455) is re-created from its 120 body with one change in the insert branch (lines 531–533): when **no Confirmed or Validated enrollment of the group's programme and year** exists for the learner, a caller whose stored role is `receptionist` gets `23514`, hint `eap.paid_enrollment_required`, message « Inscrivez et encaissez d'abord : l'affectation suit une inscription payée. ». After this release such an enrollment exists only through a payment, the Q1 exception, a management placement with a reason or a director reopen (the K1 rows the director reviews in Contrôles), so the check reads the enrollment status and does not look up receipts. A `director`/`admin` caller must supply **a dedicated reason** through a **new parameter `p_without_enrollment_reason text default null`** (1–200 characters) on `academic_security.place` and on the public command, distinct from `p_capacity_override_reason`: one reason never grants both a capacity override and an enrollment-less placement, and the management UI asks for each separately (« Motif de l'affectation sans inscription payée » and, when the group is full, « Motif du dépassement »). The insert is logged through `academic_security.log('placement_without_enrollment', …)` with the reason. **Function identities (S1):** adding a parameter changes the identity of both functions, so the migration `drop function academic_security.place(uuid, uuid, uuid, text, timestamptz, timestamptz, text, boolean)` and `drop function public.place_learner_in_group(uuid, uuid, timestamptz, uuid, text, timestamptz, text)`, creates each again with `p_without_enrollment_reason text default null` as its **last** parameter, re-applies the revokes and grants explicitly (a new function is executable by `PUBLIC` by default): `revoke all … from public, anon, authenticated, service_role` on the private core, and `revoke all … from public, anon` + `grant execute … to authenticated` on the public command, as 120 did; then `notify pgrst, 'reload schema'`. Without the drops the two existing call sites, `place_learner_in_group` (120 line 674) and `assign_receptionist_student_group` (120 lines 1000 and 1015), would fail with "function … is not unique"; with them, their eight-argument positional calls resolve to the single new signature through the default, so `assign_receptionist_student_group` (reception only, passes no reason) needs no re-creation. Callers of the public command use named arguments. **Between migration and deploy** the old build cannot send the reason, so a director/admin placement of a learner without an enrollment is refused for those minutes (reception is refused by design); every other placement works. The rehearsal asserts the ACLs of both new functions equal the 120 ones. Every reception placement path reaches this branch and is covered without further change: the group page « Ajouter des apprenants » and the learner file (`place_learner_in_group`), the Students list « Groupe à affecter » and the learner file for reception (`assign_receptionist_student_group`, 120 line 979, which calls `place(…, null, …)`), and the learner form at creation (`StudentForm.jsx` places through `placeLearnerInGroup`). Placement of an existing Confirmed/Validated enrollment is unchanged.

**CRM prospect (prospect panel).** « Commencer l'inscription » becomes « Inscrire et encaisser », a three-step dialog: (1) learner identity exactly as today (candidate review, linked learner preselected, birth date rule); (2) enrollment: programme, school year, level, group optional (no « Démarrage » choice); (3) payment: the same fields as the receipt form (price, discount with reason, payment amount, date, method, transaction reference, e-mail opt-in), with the desk-guard preview. Submit calls one new command:

- `public.crm_enroll_and_collect(p_request_key uuid, p_data jsonb) returns jsonb`, SECURITY DEFINER, fixed `search_path`, executable by `authenticated` only (the 084 revoke/grant pattern), with `crm_security.require_reader(false)` and `operational_security.require_capability('manage_finance_operations')` (reception, admin, director). `p_data` = `{enrollment: {…the crm_start_enrollment payload minus initial_status and group_id…}, payment: {…the create_charge_payment payload minus student_id, enrollment_id, session_type, school_year, level and idempotency_key…}}`, bounded (16 KB) and key-checked like the inner commands. The `payment` object is **absent** only in the Q12 case (linking an already paid enrollment).
- Body, one transaction: (0) the wrapper takes the same transaction-level advisory lock as `crm_start_enrollment` (`crm:request:<actor>:start_enrollment:<p_request_key>`; the inner command re-acquires it harmlessly in the same transaction) and **then** reads `crm_command_requests` for (`actor`, `start_enrollment`, `p_request_key`): a row means a replay (`replayed_enrollment = true`); a concurrent second first-send therefore waits on the lock and, once the first commits, reads the stored request and returns the original result instead of running the payment check; (i) `crm_start_enrollment(p_request_key, enrollment || {initial_status: 'Confirmed'})` — on a replay this returns the stored result; otherwise the lead is locked, the learner chosen or created, the Confirmed enrollment inserted (or an existing one linked, Q12) and linked, `evaluate_conversion` converts the lead and cancels its open tasks (decision 4; the follow-up branch of the function finds the lead CONVERTED and creates nothing); (ii) when a `payment` object is sent: `create_charge_payment(payment || {student_id, enrollment_id, session_type, school_year, level} taken from the enrollment result || {idempotency_key: <derived>})` — the receipt is issued and linked to that enrollment, or the committed request replays by its derived key; (iii) **the B1 check, on a first run only (R1):** the wrapper raises `22023`, hint `eap.payment_required`, message « L'inscription se confirme à l'encaissement : saisissez un paiement. », unless the payment result carries a `receipt_id` **or** `free_enrollment = true` (Q1). Before calling the inner command it also refuses `payment_amount <= 0` unless the discount equals the gross price. **On a replay (step 0 found the request) the check is skipped**: the original run already proved the receipt or the free enrollment, and the payment replay result of the inner command does not carry the enrollment keys (122 lines 630–638), so a resend after a lost response returns the original ids without raising, for a normal and for a free enrollment alike. **The Q12 check (R2), when no `payment` object is sent:** the wrapper reads the enrollment step (i) linked and raises `eap.payment_required` unless that enrollment is Confirmed/Validated **and** either a live receipt with `montant_paye > 0` is linked to it (`receipts.enrollment_id = e.id`, or `receipts.charge_id` in its charges), `voided_at` and `deleted_at` null, **or** a non-voided charge linked to it is fully discounted (`gross_amount > 0 and discount_amount = gross_amount`); an unpaid Confirmed/Validated row (a management placement, a reopen, a K1 row) is refused, so the lead is not converted on an unpaid enrollment. A raise rolls back the whole transaction: enrollment, lead link, `lead_converted` activity, cancelled tasks and the `crm_command_requests` row; (iv) returns the lead result, `enrollment` (with its `updated_at`), `charge_id`, `receipt_id`, `balance`, `free_enrollment`, `replayed_enrollment` (from step 0) and `replayed_payment` (from the inner result).
- **Idempotency (S4):** both inner commands keep their own identity. The payment's `idempotency_key` is **derived inside the wrapper** from the caller and the CRM request key (`uuid` built from `sha256('eap:payment:' || auth.uid()::text || ':' || p_request_key::text)`, version-5 style), never accepted from the client, so a resend with the same request key by the same user can only replay the same receipt and can never issue a second one, and another user's resend of a copied key is a distinct financial request that the CRM request check refuses first (step 0 is per actor scope); a resend whose payment payload differs raises the existing fingerprint conflict (the Q7 French wording of Premium retirement), with nothing written. The wrapper stores nothing itself.
- **Atomicity:** a desk-guard refusal, a payment error or the B1 check rolls back the enrollment, the lead link and the conversion: no enrollment ever exists without its receipt (or its 100 % discount, Q1, or the paid enrollment it links, Q12).
- **Locks and the loser's error:** `crm_start_enrollment` takes the exclusive enrollment-intent lock, then `create_charge_payment` takes the shared one in the same transaction (a session never conflicts with its own advisory locks); students are locked `for update` by both. Two wrappers on one lead: the second either loses the lead version check (`PT409`, hint `crm_enrollment.lead_changed`) or, when it started after the winner committed, meets `lead_already_enrolled` (`22023`): both are "refresh the prospect" in the dialog, and the test asserts one receipt and one enrollment whichever error the loser gets. A wrapper against the receipt form on one learner: `PT409` on the enrollment-intent lock for the loser.
