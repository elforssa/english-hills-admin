# Owner summary

**Online learning, pilot.** English Hills teaches some adult groups and one-to-one lessons online, inside the platform, under its own brand, with the same logins, student and parent portals, receipts and finance as today. Reception enrols and collects payment exactly as for an in-person group. Teachers never see a family's contact details.

## What will change

- **Two new course types**, `Online` and `Online One-to-One`, join the existing programme list (Yearly, Adults, …) everywhere a programme is chosen: group, learner, enrolment, charge and receipt. An online receipt reads `Online · 2026/2027` through the existing finance rule; nothing else in finance changes. The public registration form keeps today's eight programmes: nobody signs up for an online course without reception.
- **An online group gets real dated lessons.** The director sets a weekly pattern for a term (for example Monday and Wednesday 18:00–19:30, Casablanca time) and the platform creates one lesson per date, each with its own status (planned, cancelled, done) and its own teacher, so a substitute can be set for one lesson. Edits ask “this lesson only” or “this and the following ones”.
- **A “Rejoindre le cours” button** appears in the student portal (adults) and in the parent portal (children) from 15 minutes before the lesson. It opens the lesson inside an English Hills page; the video comes from a provider's embedded room, with the provider's branding hidden as far as the chosen provider allows. There is no meeting link to share: the button obtains a short-lived pass valid only for that logged-in person and that lesson.
- **The teacher's online space** (in the teacher portal) lists the teacher's lessons, opens the room, shows the roster as first name and initial only, records attendance per lesson and a two-line lesson report.
- **Cancellations and reschedules** by the office notify every affected family in the portal and by e-mail.
- **Seat capacity is enforced by the database** for online groups (8–10 by default), and reception can add a trial learner to a single lesson.
- **Children's groups** can be opened only once the learner's video-consent line is recorded by reception; the platform refuses to open a child's room without it.

## What staff/users will be able to do

- **Director / admin:** create an online group and its course record, set the teacher, the weekly pattern, the term and the capacity; activate the course (which creates the provider room); cancel or reschedule lessons; set a substitute; see every roster, attendance and lesson report; read how many lessons each online teacher completed in a month.
- **Receptionist:** enrol learners into online groups, from the learner file or from a qualified CRM lead exactly as today (the reception commands that today refuse any unknown programme are extended by one line each to accept the two new ones, D1), and take payment as today; add a trial learner or an override to one lesson; record a child's video consent; see rosters and attendance; cancel or reschedule a lesson (notifying families).
- **Teacher (online):** see own lessons, join the room as host, see the roster as first name + initial, level and attendance, mark attendance, write the lesson report, contact the office. Nothing else.
- **Adult learner:** see upcoming online lessons in the student portal and join from a phone or a desktop browser.
- **Parent:** see each child's upcoming online lessons in the parent portal and open the child's room on the parent's device.

## What remains restricted

- **Online teachers see no contact data at all**: no e-mail, phone, address, birth date, parent name or learner file, in any screen, API or database read. This is enforced structurally: an online group never carries a teacher in the field that grants in-person teachers their access, and online teachers read only through narrow projections.
- No teacher can cancel a lesson, change a pattern or add a guest; the office does.
- No shareable link, no recording, no homework, no materials library, no WhatsApp automation, no self-serve sign-up, no online payment, no mobile app, no analytics, no in-person attendance change, no change to CRM constraints, Meta mappings or the funnel (Q2 = option C: a CRM lead is enrolled into an online group through the existing CRM path and keeps its own programme).
- The provider receives only a display name (first name + initial), a role and an opaque reference; never an e-mail, phone, birth date or level.
- The receptionist still cannot see finance analytics, payroll or integrations; provider configuration is director-only.

## UI impact

- New staff pages `/online` (online courses) and `/online/<group>` (course: pattern, lessons, roster, guests, consent, attendance), a sidebar entry “Cours en ligne”.
- New portal sections: `/teacher-portal/online` and `/teacher-portal/online/<lesson>`, `/student-portal/online`, `/parent-portal/online`; one room page `/online/room/<lesson>` shared by every role, phone-first.
- The group form hides the teacher field for online course types and points to the course page; the programme selects gain the two new values; the director's `/crm/analytics` programme view is unchanged: an online enrolment counts under the lead's own programme (Q2 = C).

## Database impact

One additive, forward-only migration (number taken at implementation; see [Migration numbering](#migration-numbering-verified-2026-10-17)): the course-type vocabulary extended on `groups`, `students`, `enrollments`, `charges` and `receipts` ; no CRM constraint, mapping or cohort-function change, but one CRM function, `crm_start_enrollment`, has its accepted-programme array extended by the two values so the existing CRM enrolment path can reach an online group (Q2 = C1, confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-18); two receptionist objects from migration 096, the programme rule `operational_security.valid_session_level` and the command `save_receptionist_enrollment`, are re-created from their 096 bodies with only their hard-coded programme lists extended by the same two values, so reception's learner, group and enrolment commands accept them (B3); seven new tables (`online_courses`, `online_lesson_patterns`, `online_lessons`, `online_lesson_guests`, `online_attendance`, `online_join_tokens`, `online_consents`) plus the optional `online_join_events`, with RLS, a private `online_security` schema and narrow RPCs; triggers on `groups` (no teacher on an online group; online course type kept), on `online_courses` (its group has an online course type) and on `enrollments` (online seat capacity at group assignment); two new notification types appended to the existing list. No existing row is rewritten, no existing policy is widened, in-person attendance is untouched, and nothing depends on the Premium tables that Release B will drop.

## Important security decisions

1. **Teacher boundary by structure, not by hiding fields.** Online groups keep `groups.teacher_id` NULL (trigger-enforced), so the existing teacher RLS rules, which read full learner rows for a teacher's groups, grant an online teacher nothing. The online teacher is recorded on the course and on each lesson and reads rosters only through a projection RPC that returns first name + initial, level and attendance.
2. **Join authorization is server-side and per lesson.** A join pass is issued by a SECURITY DEFINER RPC that checks the caller's stored role, the caller's link to the learner (the same rule the portals already use), the enrolment or guest row, the lesson state, the join window, capacity and, for children, consent. The provider token is minted on the server from that pass, expires at lesson end, and is never a durable room link.
3. **Provider behind an adapter, secret on the server only.** The provider API key exists only as a Vercel Production Secret referenced by name; the database stores an opaque room reference. A server live gate keeps every online API route (join, activation, lesson actions, webhook) closed until the owner's separate activation approval, and the fake provider is refused in Production.
4. **Minimal data to the provider:** display name (first name + initial), role, expiry, opaque participant reference. Provider join/leave data is evidence only; attendance is written only by the teacher or staff.
5. **Every new route is wired in middleware, the client guard, the route policy and the sidebar, with the middleware and navigation tests extended**; API handlers authenticate themselves.

## Risks / owner review points

1. **The course-type vocabulary is wider than the brief says.** The list lives in thirteen places (five table constraints, two CRM constraints, the director cohort function, the accepted-programme array inside `crm_start_enrollment`, the CRM level rule, the receptionist programme rule and the inline list inside `save_receptionist_enrollment` (both migration 096), and the shared programme list, which the public registration form also consumes). Q1 decides the model; Q2 = C keeps the CRM constraints, mappings and cohort function untouched but needs the single array extension in `crm_start_enrollment`, which the owner confirmed as C1 on 2026-10-18; the two 096 lists are extended likewise, one line each (B3, Q16), and the public form keeps a subset without the online types (I5, Q15).
2. **The in-person teacher portal still exposes contact e-mails of a teacher's own learners** (verified at [teacher-portal/page.jsx:527](../../../src/app/(admin)/teacher-portal/page.jsx)). This plan protects online teachers only; the in-person gap stays a separate item.
3. **Link secrecy depends on the provider.** An embedded room's address is visible in the browser's developer tools; secrecy must rest on short-lived, per-person passes and a room that refuses tokenless joins. One room serves every lesson of a course, so a pass is scoped to a lesson in time (its expiry), not in space; the provider must honour expiry server-side (T-P7). The provider test must prove this (acceptance tests T-P1 to T-P7) before the real adapter is implemented.
4. **Display-name immutability also depends on the provider.** The test must prove participants cannot rename themselves.
5. **Children.** The consent gate is technical; the wording and the date are the owner's and the lawyer's (Q10).
6. **Notification type constraint collision with Premium Release B.** Both migrations rewrite `notifications_type_check`; whichever lands second must rebuild it from the then-current list (stop condition S4).
7. **Email linkage of families is unchanged.** Parents and learners are matched by e-mail today; the join rule reuses it. A stronger account link is out of scope.
8. **Open provider decision.** The 30-minute phone test (10 people, Moroccan 4G, plus desktop) decides the provider; the adapter is designed so the choice does not change the plan.

- **The retired Premium workshop model** (051–054) is not a precedent for online rooms and must not be revived or reused.

### Portals

- The teacher, parent and student portals are single client pages reading through the shared [entity wrappers](../../../src/lib/entities.js) under RLS. Parents resolve their learners by `students.parent_email`, students by `students.email`; the database rule `get_visible_student_ids()` ([006](../../../supabase/migrations/006_rls_policies.sql)) is the same e-mail match.
- Communication primitives: `announcements` (audience all/group/parents/teachers), `messages` (direct, e-mail-addressed, authorized by `can_message_recipient`), `notifications` (in-portal rows with read state from [028](../../../supabase/migrations/028_notification_read_state.sql); fixed types from 001/052 including `schedule_change` and `class_reminder`; rows are created by staff pages and by database triggers). None carries a meeting, a room or a join window; there is no portal timetable.

### Storage, integrations and infrastructure

- Files go through the storage asset registry ([045](../../../supabase/migrations/045_storage_asset_registry.sql), [046](../../../supabase/migrations/046_storage_authorization_enforcement.sql)); the pilot adds no file purpose (no recording, no materials).
- Server-side e-mail is Resend through `sendEmail` in [email.js](../../../src/lib/email.js) and the authenticated `/api/email/send` route; local development disables it.
- Scheduled work follows one pattern (pg_cron + pg_net to a protected endpoint with a dedicated bearer and a server gate). **The pilot needs no scheduler**: lessons are generated when the pattern is saved and rooms are created at activation.
- Secrets exist only as server environment variables or Vercel Production Secrets referenced by name.
- **No video, meeting or calendar dependency exists** ([package.json](../../../package.json)); Supabase Realtime is enabled locally but unused by the application.
- Vercel Functions run in `dub1` beside the Supabase project in eu-west-1; Sentry is errors-only.

## Scope and non-goals

**In the pilot (one adult group of 6–10, one or two one-to-one learners, one or two teachers, 4–6 weeks; a children's group when the owner decides):**

- Online course types, online course record, weekly pattern → dated lessons, per-lesson teacher and substitute, cancel/reschedule with notifications.
- Join from the student portal (adults) and the parent portal (children), phone-first and desktop; teacher host join; a shared room page embedding the provider's room behind the adapter.
- Teacher roster by projection, online attendance per lesson, two-line lesson report.
- Server-enforced capacity, per-lesson trial guests and overrides, child consent gate, payment warning on the staff roster.
- Director read of completed lessons per teacher per month (input to the existing manual payroll).

**Out of the pilot:** recording; homework; materials library; WhatsApp notifications; online payment or self-serve sign-up; a mobile app; analytics; any change to in-person attendance, groups or the teacher portal's existing behaviour; chat filtering or moderation; a stronger family-account link; CRM campaign attribution for online programmes (Q2); automatic payroll.

## Design

### D1 — Course types

Add `Online` and `Online One-to-One` to the shared vocabulary. The migration rebuilds, from their latest cumulative definitions, `groups_session_type_check` (049), `students_session_type_check` (049), `enrollments_session_type_check` (072), `receipts_session_type_check` (055) and the `charges` session-type check (055). `SESSION_TYPES` and `LEVELS_BY_SESSION` in [academicPrograms.js](../../../src/lib/academicPrograms.js) gain both values (levels per Q3; default CEFR A1–C2). Finance needs no function change: [113](../../../supabase/migrations/113_premium_retirement_finance_contract.sql) already builds `<session type> · <school year>` for every non-Yearly, non-Other charge, so an online charge reads `Online · 2026/2027`; receipts copy it. **Q2 = option C (owner, 2026-10-17):** the CRM constraints (078, 086), the Meta mappings and the cohort function (116) are untouched; leads keep the existing programme list, and the programme view attributes an online enrolment to the lead's own programme. The CRM enrolment dialog ([CrmEnrollmentDialog](../../../src/components/crm/CrmEnrollmentDialog.jsx)) offers the two types automatically because it reads `SESSION_TYPES`; its programme label map gains the two labels. No refusal and no dialog filter exist in this design. **Repository fact that qualifies C:** `crm_start_enrollment` ([112](../../../supabase/migrations/112_crm_rcc_a2_enrollment_ux.sql) line 51) refuses any programme outside a hard-coded eight-value array before it looks at the group, and requires the chosen group's `session_type` to equal the requested programme (line 106); conversion fires only for the enrolment that this function links to the lead ([084](../../../supabase/migrations/084_crm_enrollment_and_conversion.sql), `crm_leads.enrollment_id`), so an enrolment made from the learner file does not convert the lead. The CRM path can therefore reach an online group only if that one array gains the two values: the migration re-creates `crm_start_enrollment` from its latest cumulative body with that single change and nothing else (signature, grants, hints, locks, replay and follow-up logic unchanged), proven by a catalog diff of exactly one line. `crm_security.valid_enrollment_level` needs no change under Q3-A because it already treats every other programme as CEFR; Q3-B would change it. **The owner confirmed this reading as C1 on 2026-10-18** ([Q2](#owner-decisions-required)): only that array changes; no other CRM function, constraint, lead field, Meta mapping or funnel object. **Correction of the review finding:** the reviewer's B1 stated that option C needed zero CRM change. That was incorrect, because the accepted-programme list is a literal array inside the function body (112 line 51), not a table constraint or a lookup: no constraint, mapping or data change can make the function accept a value it refuses in code, and no other path links an enrolment to a lead. The plan therefore records the one-line function change as part of C, with the unchanged-from-112 proof below.

**Receptionist commands (B3, OL-r4).** Migration [096](../../../supabase/migrations/096_receptionist_operational_permissions.sql) hard-codes the same eight programmes twice more: in `operational_security.valid_session_level(p_session text, p_level text)` (lines 45–50; `language sql`, `immutable`, `security invoker`, `search_path = pg_catalog, pg_temp`, executable by no API role because line 53 revokes every function of the schema), which `save_receptionist_student` (line 160), `save_receptionist_group` (lines 225 and 258) and `save_receptionist_enrollment` (lines 307 and 313) call, and in an inline list inside `save_receptionist_enrollment` (lines 309–312, raising `Invalid enrollment session/year`). No migration after 096 re-creates either function, and the receptionist UI calls exactly these commands ([EnrollmentModal.jsx:44](../../../src/components/students/EnrollmentModal.jsx), [ReceptionistStudentForm.jsx:63](../../../src/components/students/ReceptionistStudentForm.jsx), [students/page.jsx:70](../../../src/app/(admin)/students/page.jsx), [groups/page.jsx:41](../../../src/app/(admin)/groups/page.jsx)). Without a change, reception could not set a learner's programme to `Online`, create an online group or enrol a learner into one from the learner file, contradicting the owner summary. The migration therefore re-creates both from their 096 bodies with `create or replace function` under the identical signature: `valid_session_level` gains `'Online','Online One-to-One'` appended to the last line of its `p_session in (…)` list (its `else` branch already returns CEFR A1–C2 for every programme other than Yearly and Adults, consistent with Q3-A; Q3-B would add a `when` branch here as well as in `crm_security.valid_enrollment_level`), and `save_receptionist_enrollment` gains the same two values appended to the last line of its inline list (line 310) and nothing else: the same nine-parameter signature, `security definer`, `search_path` and body otherwise, with the 096 privileges (revoked from every role, executable by `authenticated`) preserved because `create or replace` keeps ownership and privileges. Each is proven unchanged-from-096 by the same catalog diff as `crm_start_enrollment` ([migration strategy](#migration-strategy)): exactly one changed line per function. `save_receptionist_student` and `save_receptionist_group` are not re-created: they carry no inline list and accept the new programmes through the helper. The alternative, leaving 096 untouched, would mean that reception's learner-file path cannot reach online groups in the pilot (only the CRM path and the director/admin forms could), and the owner summary would have to say so; it is not recommended and is the owner's call under [Q16](#owner-decisions-required).

**Public registration form (I5, OL-r4).** `SESSION_TYPES` is also consumed by the public form `/inscription` ([page.jsx:209](../../../src/app/inscription/page.jsx)) and by its API schema ([route.js:26](../../../src/app/api/public/inscription/route.js), `z.enum(SESSION_TYPES)`), so extending the shared list alone would offer and accept the online programmes to anonymous visitors, against “no self-serve sign-up”. Decision: `academicPrograms.js` additionally exports `PUBLIC_SESSION_TYPES`, today's eight programmes without the two online values; the public page's select and the API's `z.enum` consume it instead of `SESSION_TYPES`, so a direct POST with `Online` is refused by validation like any unknown value. Every staff screen (group form, learner forms, receptionist forms, CRM enrolment dialog, receipt form, import, reports) keeps `SESSION_TYPES`. A node unit test (`scripts/test-public-programs.mjs`, added to `npm test`) asserts that `PUBLIC_SESSION_TYPES` equals `SESSION_TYPES` without `Online` and `Online One-to-One` and that the public inscription schema refuses both values while accepting `Adults`; because the repository's node tests import `.mjs` modules (as [navigation.mjs](../../../src/lib/navigation.mjs) is), the implementer exposes the two lists through an `.mjs` module re-exported by `academicPrograms.js`. If the owner prefers the public form to offer the online programmes, [Q15](#owner-decisions-required) records that acceptance and the subset is dropped.

### D2 — Online course record

`online_courses` extends a group one-to-one: `group_id` (PK, FK `groups`), `teacher_id` (FK `teachers`, required), `audience` (`adult` | `child`), `capacity` (1–12, default 10; One-to-One groups default 1), `lesson_minutes`, `join_open_minutes` (default 15), `join_grace_minutes` (default 10), `provider` (adapter id), `provider_room_ref` (opaque, NULL until activation; never a URL), `status` (`draft` | `active` | `closed`), `term_start`, `term_end`, `version`, audit columns. A trigger on `groups` raises when a group whose `session_type` is online has a non-NULL `teacher_id` (reason `online.teacher_on_course`) and when the `session_type` of a group that has an `online_courses` row changes to a non-online value (reason `online.course_type_locked`); a trigger on `online_courses` (insert and update of `group_id`) raises unless that group's `session_type` is online (reason `online.group_not_online`). A CHECK constraint cannot read another table, so both rules are triggers and both are rehearsed. The group form hides the teacher field for online types and links to the course page. `groups.jours`/`horaire` are overwritten with a derived text (for example `Lun, Mer · 18:00–19:30`) whenever the pattern is saved, so the existing group list and timetable keep showing something true.

### D3 — Pattern and lessons

- `online_lesson_patterns`: `id`, `group_id`, `weekday` (0–6), `start_time` (Casablanca civil time), `minutes`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`, `version`. Several rows per course (Monday and Wednesday).
- `online_lessons`: `id`, `group_id`, `pattern_id` (nullable), `starts_at`/`ends_at` (`timestamptz`), `status` (`scheduled` | `cancelled` | `completed`), `teacher_id` (effective teacher; defaults to the course teacher), `is_substitute`, `cancel_reason`, `cancelled_at`, `report` (≤ 400 characters), `report_by`, `completed_at`, `detached` (edited individually), `version`, audit columns; unique `(group_id, starts_at)`.
- Civil date and time are projected by the read RPCs in `Africa/Casablanca` under the adopted migration-109 display contract; raw instants remain the identity.
- `online_save_pattern(group, patterns jsonb, from_date, version)` (management): replaces the patterns, then regenerates every `scheduled`, non-detached lesson on or after `from_date` up to `term_end` (bounded to 26 weeks); past, cancelled, completed and detached lessons are kept. Idempotent on repeat.
- `online_update_lesson(lesson, version, changes, scope)` (operations): `scope = 'this'` edits one lesson and marks it detached; `scope = 'following'` moves the pattern from that lesson's date and regenerates the following scheduled, non-detached lessons.
- `online_set_lesson_teacher(lesson, version, teacher)` sets a substitute for one lesson.
- `online_cancel_lesson(lesson, version, reason, scope)` cancels one lesson or this-and-following; cancelled lessons keep their rows, and every open join pass of a cancelled lesson is revoked (D6).
- Every command is SECURITY DEFINER with explicit stored-role checks, version checks (SQLSTATE `40001` on a stale version, reserved for stale-version only), fixed `search_path` and `authenticated` execute only; direct table writes are revoked.

### D4 — Who may join: seat rule and capacity

`online_security.can_join(lesson_id, student_id)` is true when all hold: the lesson is `scheduled`; the learner has an enrolment in the lesson's group with status Confirmed or Validated, **or** an `online_lesson_guests` row (`kind` `trial` | `override`, added by operations staff with a note) for that lesson; the instant is inside `[starts_at − join_open_minutes, ends_at + join_grace_minutes]`; and, when the course audience is `child`, an unrevoked `online_consents` row of kind `video_minor` exists for the learner. Trial enrolments alone do not qualify (Q4). Capacity is enforced **at group assignment, never inside a status transition**: a trigger on `enrollments` (before insert, before update of `group_id`, and before update of `status` only when the old status is `Rejected`) refuses placing an enrolment whose status is anything but `Rejected` into an online group when the count of that group's other non-Rejected enrolments already equals `capacity` (reason `online.capacity`). It does not fire on a status-only change, so the payment-driven confirmation inside `create_charge_payment` ([072](../../../supabase/migrations/072_paid_enrollment_without_group.sql)) and the enrolment-to-student sync of [070](../../../supabase/migrations/070_enrollment_student_atomic_sync.sql) (which writes `students.groupe_id`, never `enrollments.group_id`) never evaluate capacity. The other direction is an assignment: when `students.groupe_id` changes (the admin student form, or `assign_receptionist_student_group` from [096](../../../supabase/migrations/096_receptionist_operational_permissions.sql) called without an enrolment, line 366), the [074](../../../supabase/migrations/074_enrollment_workflow_consistency.sql) trigger `sync_enrollments_from_student` writes `enrollments.group_id` (074 line 97), and that write passes through the capacity trigger like any other: placing a learner into a full online group from the student form is refused with `online.capacity` and the student row stays unchanged, so the capacity trigger carries no trigger-depth guard. Every path that assigns a group (the admin enrolment forms, `save_receptionist_enrollment` and `assign_receptionist_student_group` from 096, the 074 student-form placement and `crm_start_enrollment`) passes through it. `online_add_guest` refuses when the group's non-Rejected enrolments plus the lesson's guests already reach `capacity`. A missed payment never blocks (D12).

### D5 — Teacher boundary (anti-poaching)

- The online teacher is `online_courses.teacher_id` (and `online_lessons.teacher_id` for a substitute); `groups.teacher_id` stays NULL (D2). Consequently none of the 071 teacher rules, the 006 group-scoped teacher policies, the attendance trigger's teacher branch or `can_message_recipient`'s teacher branch grants the online teacher anything: no `students` row, no enrolment, no e-mail recipient other than the office.
- Teacher reads go through `online_list_my_lessons(from, to)` and `online_get_lesson_roster(lesson)`, which return `student_id`, `display_label`, `niveau_cefr`, this lesson's attendance status and the teacher's own attendance note, and a guest flag (never the staff member's guest note); never `full_name`, e-mail, phone, birth date, address, parent fields or balance. `display_label` is built by the private helper `online_security.student_label(student_id)`: first given-name token plus the initial of the last token of `students.full_name`, with a numeric suffix when two labels in the same course collide.
- Roster access window: a teacher sees a lesson from `teacher_access_before` (24 h before `starts_at`) until `teacher_access_after` (7 days after `ends_at`), only while the course is `active` and the teacher is the course teacher or that lesson's teacher. Outside the window the RPC returns nothing. A substitute therefore sees exactly one lesson's roster.
- The teacher's display name to the provider is the safe teacher directory name (first name + initial), never the e-mail.
- Messaging: the pilot keeps only the provider's in-room chat (Q11) and office-sent announcements; direct teacher-to-learner messaging outside the room is Q13 (recommended: not in the pilot). The existing teacher portal messaging already limits an online teacher to the office.

### D6 — Join flow and room page

- Portal lists use `online_list_lessons_for_me()` (student) and `online_list_lessons_for_my_children()` (parent), returning lesson identity, civil date/time, course name, teacher label, status and the server-computed `join_opens_at` / `join_closes_at`. The button is enabled only inside that window; the server re-checks on issue.
- `/online/room/<lesson>` (plus `?student=<id>` for a parent) is a client page for every operational role. It POSTs to `/api/online/join` `{ lesson, student? }`. The handler authenticates the session, calls `online_issue_join(lesson, student)` as the caller (SECURITY DEFINER: resolves the participant kind from the stored role and the learner link via `public.get_visible_student_ids()` (schema-qualified) for parents and students, the lesson's effective teacher for teachers, director/admin for observers under Q6; evaluates `can_join` for learners; inserts an `online_join_tokens` row with `jti`, `participant_kind`, `student_id`/`teacher_id`/`profile_id`, `display_label`, `issued_at`, `expires_at = ends_at + grace`), then mints the provider token through the adapter with `{ roomRef, displayName: display_label, role: host | participant, expiresAt, participantRef: jti }` and returns `{ provider, embed }`. The page mounts the provider's embedded room with that token. Re-issue for reconnection is allowed and logged; nothing durable is ever returned.
- **Windows.** Learners and parents may obtain a pass inside `[starts_at − join_open_minutes, ends_at + join_grace_minutes]`. Hosts (the lesson's effective teacher) have their own window, `[starts_at − host_open_minutes (default 30), ends_at + join_grace_minutes]`; observers (Q6) use the learner window. Outside the window the RPC refuses (`online.window_closed`).
- **Temporal scoping.** One room serves every lesson of a course, so a pass is scoped to a lesson by time, not by room: it expires at `ends_at + grace`, and lessons of one course never overlap (the pattern generator and `online_update_lesson` refuse overlapping lessons in the same course). A pass for lesson A therefore cannot be used during lesson B. The provider must honour the expiry server-side (T-P7).
- **Revocation.** `online_cancel_lesson`, `online_remove_guest` and `online_close_course` set `revoked_at` on every open pass they affect; the join route refuses to re-issue for a revoked pass, the room page re-reads lesson state every 30 seconds and leaves when the lesson is cancelled or the learner no longer qualifies, and the adapter's optional `revokeParticipant(roomRef, participantRef)` ejects the participant where the provider supports it. Where it does not, the residual exposure is bounded by the pass expiry; the implementation record states which case the chosen provider is.
- Everything is refused with a reason hint (`online.<reason>`, closed vocabulary, no data) when the live gate is off, the course is not active, the lesson is cancelled, the window is closed, the seat rule fails, consent is missing or capacity is exceeded; the page shows French text per reason, never raw server text.

### D7 — Join evidence

`online_join_tokens` is the primary evidence (who was issued a pass, when, and whether it was revoked). Where the chosen adapter declares webhook support, `/api/webhooks/online/<provider>` verifies the signature through the adapter before any write and appends `online_join_events` (`token_id`, `joined` | `left`, `at`, opaque provider participant reference); for any other provider name, for the fake provider in Production, and whenever the live gate is off, the route answers 404 and writes nothing. Evidence is shown on the staff and teacher rosters (“a rejoint à 18:02”) and **never writes attendance**.

### D8 — Online attendance and lesson report

`online_attendance` (`lesson_id`, `student_id`, `status` Présent/Absent/Retard/Justifié, `note` ≤ 200 characters, `marked_by`, `marked_at`; unique per lesson and learner). `online_save_attendance(lesson, version, rows jsonb, report)` is callable by the lesson's effective teacher inside the access window and by operations staff; it upserts rows for enrolled learners and guests of that lesson, stores the report, and sets the lesson `completed` once called after `starts_at`. In-person `attendance` is untouched. `online_teacher_lesson_counts(month)` (director/admin) returns completed lessons and minutes per teacher as an input to the manual payroll (Q8).

### D9 — Cancellations, reschedules and notifications

`online_cancel_lesson` / `online_update_lesson` (operations staff) write one `notifications` row per affected recipient address (the learner's e-mail and the parent's e-mail when present) with the new types `online_lesson_cancelled` / `online_lesson_rescheduled`, in French, naming only the course, old and new civil time and reason; the rows carry the in-portal read state. The same staff action route (`/api/online/lessons/<action>`, director/admin/receptionist, authenticates itself) then sends the e-mails through the server `sendEmail` helper to addresses it resolves with the privileged client and marks `sent`; the teacher never triggers this and never sees the addresses. WhatsApp is out of scope. The `notifications_type_check` constraint is rebuilt by copying its current list **verbatim** (today the 001 list plus `premium_homework` from 052) and appending the two online types; it never removes a value, so every existing row, including any `premium_homework` row, stays valid. The rehearsal in the migration strategy proves it, and stop condition S4 covers both landing orders with Premium retirement Release B.

### D10 — Children and consent

`online_consents` (`student_id`, `kind` = `video_minor`, `recorded_at`, `recorded_by`, `note`, `revoked_at`), written by operations staff through `online_record_consent` after the signed consent line (wording by the lawyer; Q10). `can_join` requires an unrevoked row for any learner of a `child` course; the course page shows which enrolled learners lack it. No consent text or document is stored in the pilot.

### D11 — Provider adapter and configuration

- Interface in `src/lib/online/provider/index.mjs`: `createRoom({ courseRef, audience })` → `{ roomRef }`; `closeRoom(roomRef)`; `mintJoinToken({ roomRef, displayName, role, expiresAt, participantRef })` → `{ token, embed }`; `verifyWebhook(request)` → events. - Server-only configuration: `ONLINE_VIDEO_PROVIDER` (`fake` | `whereby` | `daily` | `zoom-video`), `ONLINE_VIDEO_PROVIDER_API_KEY` (Vercel Production Secret, referenced by name only), `ONLINE_VIDEO_PROVIDER_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (optional), and the live gate `ONLINE_LEARNING_LIVE_ENABLED` (`true` only after the owner's activation approval; every `/api/online/*` route and the activation command fail closed without it). None is `NEXT_PUBLIC_`.
- Room per course. Activation is an API route, `/api/online/courses/activate` (director/admin; authenticates itself; refuses when the live gate is off or the provider is `fake` in Production): it calls the adapter's `createRoom` and then `online_activate_course` as the caller to store the opaque reference and set the course `active`; `/api/online/courses/close` mirrors it with `closeRoom` and `online_close_course`, which also revokes every open pass. The database RPCs refuse a room reference that is not opaque (no `://`).
- **Production refuses the fake provider.** When `VERCEL_ENV` is `production`, every `/api/online/*` route, the activation route and the webhook route answer with `online.provider_invalid` if `ONLINE_VIDEO_PROVIDER` is `fake` or unset; the fake adapter serves local development, CI and preview deployments only. The provider configuration must make the room private: no join without a token minted by our server, and participants cannot change their display name. Branding: the provider's white-label options are applied as far as the plan allows; residual provider branding is an accepted pilot limitation to be recorded at activation.
- Provider acceptance tests, run by the owner and the implementer before the real adapter is approved: **T-P1** a bare room address does not join; **T-P2** a token for lesson A cannot enter lesson B's room; **T-P3** an expired token is refused; **T-P4** the display name cannot be changed by the participant; **T-P5** ten phones on Moroccan 4G plus one desktop hold a 30-minute call at acceptable quality; **T-P6** the cost per participant-minute is known in USD; **T-P7** a pass issued for lesson A is refused by the provider after its expiry although the course room still exists, and a participant ejected through `revokeParticipant` (where supported) cannot rejoin with the same pass.

### D14 — Where this sits in the platform

The online module is a sibling of academics: it reuses identity, roles, enrolment, finance and the portals, and adds lessons, rooms, join authorization and online attendance. It does not touch the CRM lifecycle, conversion, finance authority, Meta integrations or the in-person teacher model. Everything lives behind the `online_*` tables, the `online_security` schema, `src/lib/online`, `src/app/(admin)/online` and the portal sub-pages.

## Security boundaries

- Stored profile roles only; every new RPC checks the caller's role and relationship explicitly; `anon`, `public` and non-listed roles are denied; direct API grants on the `online_*` tables are revoked, with read policies only for director/admin/receptionist on courses, patterns, lessons, guests, consents and attendance (never on tokens or events); every other role reads through RPCs.
- SECURITY DEFINER functions with fixed `search_path = pg_catalog, pg_temp`, `authenticated` execute only; private helpers in `online_security` non-executable by API roles (the CRM pattern).
- Online teachers: no `students`, `enrollments`, `receipts`, `charge_balances`, `authorized_adults`, `messages` or `notifications` access beyond today's role baseline; roster projections carry no contact or finance field; a regression test asserts the projection's key set and that a teacher with an online course and no in-person group reads zero `students` rows.
- Join passes: server-issued, per person, per lesson, expiring at lesson end + grace; the provider token is minted server-side from a secret that never reaches the browser; no durable room address is returned by any API; webhook signatures verified before any write.
- Data minimisation to the provider: display label, role, expiry, opaque reference. No e-mail, phone, birth date, level, notes or attendance.
- Children: consent gate enforced in `can_join`; child display labels follow the same first-name-plus-initial rule.
- Routes: middleware, client guard, policy map and sidebar agree; API handlers authenticate themselves; the live gate closes every online route in Production until activation approval.

- **S4** Premium retirement Release B, both orders: (a) online lands first: Release B must rebuild `notifications_type_check` from the then-current list, which includes the two online types, and this outcome's closeout records that prerequisite in the Release B plan; (b) Release B lands first: this migration copies the post-B list (without `premium_homework`) and the rehearsal's seeded row changes to an ordinary type; (c) both open at once: the owner decides the order, and the second rebases its constraint text and re-runs the carry-over rehearsal before handoff.
- **S5** The next free migration number differs from the ledger, `origin/main` or an open PR: resolve before authoring the file.
- **S6** Any need for a scheduler, a new storage purpose, recording, chat moderation, online payment or a family-account change: out of scope; reopen architecture.
- **S7** Any Production read, credential, provider account or configuration action: needs its own owner approval; the implementer never performs it.

### Docs / status reporting

Durable documents (CURRENT_STATE, ARCHITECTURE, PRODUCT_RULES, SECURITY_RULES, WORKFLOWS, FEATURE_INDEX, ADR-008) are updated at release closeout, because nothing is deployed before then.
