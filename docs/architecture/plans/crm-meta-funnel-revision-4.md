## Important security decisions

D2 applies to Intake and negative outcomes just as strictly as positive outcomes. No upload of raw downloaded but unresolved/ineligible leads merely to meet coverage. Freeze one producer owner for the entire future lead lifecycle, including all five kinds. Unknown old-producer state means no native delivery. Hold possible network outcomes and dependent later events; never resolve uncertainty by switching producer, event ID, destination, contract or epoch.

## Event ordering and safe gaps

Reconciliation reads committed canonical activities, not current lead status. Order selected commercial facts by `(occurred_at, created_at, id)` with explicit Intake-first validation; preserve original times. Reject/hold internally contradictory chronology, a newly discovered fact preceding an already dispatched later fact, unknown provenance and timestamp ties that cannot be ordered truthfully; do not invent Contacting/Engaged/Qualified prerequisites for a valid conversion. Use a private SQL candidate helper shared by reconcile and claim/begin so an earlier eligible activity not yet materialized due to the batch limit still blocks a later one.

Serialize live sending per owned lead under a shared lead/ownership lock. Permit only the earliest unsent actual selected occurrence. A known receipt unlocks its successor. Earlier pending, blocked, retry or unknown occurrence holds later occurrences. If an earlier required actual occurrence expires, is erased or becomes terminal without proven receipt, suppress later dependent occurrences with a safe predecessor-gap reason; do not jump the gap or change timestamps. Revocation/closed epoch suppress all remaining work. A missing stage that never happened is not a gap; a real missing/held stage is. No requirement to create all five events for every lead.

Reopening does not unblock an uncertain earlier attempt. Later real qualification/conversion can follow an acknowledged negative chronologically; a negative is never a positive predecessor requirement. These conservative ordering choices may reduce coverage/volume and require explicit acceptance. Meta documentation motivates prior actual-stage delivery, but does not promise exactly-once effects or impose this SQL algorithm.
