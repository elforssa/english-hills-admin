## Meta rationale and evidence

The independently re-read [research register F1–F5](../evidence/crm-meta-funnel-research-2026-09-30.md) records authoritative URLs, access/update dates and evidence limits. Key distinctions:

1. **Recommended uploads:** genuine initial/raw receipt plus stage-change history. More stages do not compensate for missing leads. Meta does not prescribe these exact English Hills stage names.
2. **Validation:** connection needs a valid attributable CRM event. Funnel analysis separately calls for 200 generated leads/month, at least 60% coverage, correct format; FAQ also calls for seven days of data and at least two stages, preferably three. These are published criteria, not verified English Hills achievement or an instruction to acquire more leads.
3. **Optimization:** Qualified/Converted are approved positive stages. Intake provides receipt/coverage. Lost/Not qualified are negative closures; exclude them from the positive configured funnel, as Meta instructs. They are not optimization targets. No inspected source establishes their marginal learning benefit.
4. **Target:** the owner approved Qualified as the initial target because it is the earliest explicit quality decision. Meta's target is an earliest stage and includes downstream stages; the system may adjust. Actual quality, frequency and delay must be assessed. Converted is trusted enrollment, never Purchase or revenue.
5. **Acceptance:** HTTP receipt, CRM recognition, funnel validation, optimization selection and advertising effectiveness are separate claims. Existing aggregate CAPI traffic proves none of the future native cohort's readiness.

Meta's general all-stage recommendation is stronger than an Intake-only coverage fix. B follows it for commercial outcomes but deliberately omits operational effort/conversation stages. This minimization tradeoff is part of the approved Option B architecture and still requires later account validation; it is not a verified Meta exception. C is a fallback to consider only if evidence shows those stages are necessary/useful. A is not proven API-invalid; its coverage is structurally more dependent on qualification rates.

All use one event/request with existing event ID and original fact timestamp, `action_source=system_generated`, `user_data={lead_id: original decimal string}`, and exactly `custom_data={event_source: crm, lead_event_source: English Hills CRM}`. Decimal lead ID must not pass through JavaScript Number. Event age ≤ seven days was revalidated in F1 and must be rechecked before seed/release; strict after-generation seconds check must hold without rounding the time forward or inventing a timestamp. Reject future times and recheck age at actual dispatch with request-timeout headroom.

**Occurrence identity:** add an immutable delivery model marker (legacy first-attainment versus revision-4 stage-entry). Preserve every existing event ID and frozen row. Keep legacy once-per-lead/kind with a partial unique index for legacy modes/models; for new live rows retain singleton uniqueness for Intake/Converted and activity/connection/kind plus provider-event-ID uniqueness for all kinds. New live Qualified/closure occurrences consume distinct real activity IDs. Prevent mixing legacy and revision-4 native models for the same opportunity via ownership/epoch admission, not merely disjoint indexes. No conversion identity change, no new outbox, no replay of previously exported first qualification. Duplicate command requests must yield the same activity and intention. A repeat real stage entry is distinct business truth, not an uncertain retry.

This costs more than adding three enum values, but avoids silently losing a second Lost/Qualified after reopening. Reopening to Contacting/Engaged itself emits no B event; Meta receives milestone history, not a continuously synchronized status snapshot. Monitor this semantic limitation; do not fabricate a positive event on reopen. If a prior negative was incorrect, do not issue an invented compensating event or erase history; hold the lead and escalate a separately reviewed correction. Once-per-kind for Qualified/closures would reverse the approved occurrence choice and requires a separate architecture decision.

## Chronological attempt ordering and independent receipt uncertainty

**Finally approved by the owner on 2026-09-30 as part of revision 4.** Do not require confirmed provider receipt of every prior actual occurrence before sending a later genuine event. Reconciliation consumes committed canonical activity history, ordered by `(occurred_at, created_at, id)` with Intake-first provenance validation; preserve original timestamps. Hold contradictory chronology, a newly discovered earlier fact after a later dispatch, unknown provenance or ties that cannot be ordered truthfully. Never manufacture Contacting/Engaged/Qualified or any other missing stage. A stage that never happened is not a predecessor gap.

Use the shared private candidate helper at reconciliation, claim and begin so an earlier actual eligible activity outside the materialized batch is not skipped. Serialize dispatch authorization per owned lead with the existing lease fences and a consistent lead/ownership lock order. This orders **attempts**, not confirmed receipts or guaranteed arrival order at Meta.

| Earlier actual occurrence | Effect on later dependent genuine events |
| --- | --- |
| Not yet attempted, blocked by eligibility, or proven not to have reached its send boundary (including safe pre-send failure/backoff) | Hold the later event until the earlier event can reach its send boundary. Never bypass evidence or fabricate a send. If it becomes permanently unsendable before that boundary, retain an explicit unattempted-predecessor hold; later events can expire under their own deadlines. |
| Active attempt with unresolved dispatch ownership/lease | Serialize until that attempt is settled or fenced by recovery; a second worker must not overtake a worker that could still initiate the earlier call. |
| Crossed the network boundary and receipt is unknown/ambiguous | Hold **that earlier identity** against automatic/manual replay. Its missing receipt alone does not block or suppress later genuine Qualified/Converted events, or other independently eligible selected occurrences. Preserve the unknown state and audit history. |
| Confirmed receipt | Later event can proceed subject to its own gates; receipt is sufficient but not necessary to pass the predecessor attempt-order check. |

The implementation distinguishes durable attempt-start/send-boundary uncertainty from confirmed receipt. A committed begin followed by crash or ambiguous RPC result may have sent and cannot be replayed without authoritative safety evidence. Reconciliation uses authoritative database attempt state and fences a stale worker before advancing successors; a stale due time, status relabel or caller-supplied “sent” flag is not proof of dispatch. Once recovery establishes the earlier attempt is durably started, settled/fenced and potentially dispatched, it may pass the attempt-order gate without claiming actual network receipt. A proven unstarted/pre-send attempt continues to hold successors. Migration 102 and the SQL/worker suites test this distinction.

Every later event independently rechecks D2, producer ownership, epoch, source identity, authentic activity/conversion, time/deadline, revocation and its own uncertainty history at dispatch. Shared revocation, closed epoch or an invalid source still blocks/suppresses affected later events for that independent reason. An earlier unknown event reaching retention cleanup or terminal age does not erase its minimal attempt-order/audit marker and must not convert its lack of receipt into a descendant-suppression rule. No cross-identity replay, new event ID or changed timestamp is allowed.

**Tradeoff:** chronological attempt ordering improves useful lifecycle coverage by allowing genuine later Qualified/Converted feedback even when an earlier receipt is lost, while preserving no-uncertain-replay for each event identity. Meta may receive a later event without the earlier event, and provider arrival order is not guaranteed; the unknown earlier outcome remains visible rather than being fabricated as success. More useful lifecycle feedback is not a guarantee of higher distinct-lead coverage or optimization performance. If applicable Meta/provider evidence later requires confirmed predecessor receipt, **stop and revise the architecture for owner review**; do not silently introduce a receipt prerequisite or resend the earlier event.
