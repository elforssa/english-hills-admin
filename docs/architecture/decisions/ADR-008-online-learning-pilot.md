## Date

2026-10-17.

## Context

The owner unparked online learning for architecture on 2026-10-17 ([decision](../../ai/OWNER_DECISIONS.md#online-learning--unparked-for-architecture-2026-10-17)). The platform has groups with free-text schedules, attendance keyed on a calendar date, teacher access derived from `groups.teacher_id` that opens full learner rows to a teacher, and portals matched by e-mail. There is no room, provider, dated lesson or join model ([architecture gap](../../ai/ARCHITECTURE.md#architecture-gaps)). The owner's goals: classes inside the platform under the English Hills brand, phone-first, same logins and finance, and teachers who never see a family's contact details so that families are not drawn into private lessons.

## Decision

1. **Online is a course type, not a separate system.** `Online` and `Online One-to-One` join the shared `session_type` vocabulary; online groups are ordinary `groups` rows extended one-to-one by `online_courses`, so enrolment, placement, receipts and the portals work unchanged.
2. **Dated lessons are first-class.** A weekly pattern generates `online_lessons` with identity, status, effective teacher and Casablanca civil projection; online attendance is keyed on the lesson, and in-person attendance is untouched.
3. **The teacher boundary is structural.** An online group never carries `groups.teacher_id`; the online teacher is recorded on the course and the lesson and reads rosters only through projection RPCs returning first name + initial, level and attendance. The existing teacher rules therefore grant an online teacher nothing.
4. **Joining is a server-issued, per-person, per-lesson pass.** A SECURITY DEFINER RPC checks role, learner link, enrolment or guest row, lesson state, join window, capacity and child consent, logs the pass, and the server mints the provider token from it; no durable room address exists for participants.
5. **The provider sits behind an adapter and a live gate.** One embedded, brandable room per course from a provider chosen by a phone-and-desktop test; the secret is a Vercel Production Secret referenced by name; the provider receives only a display label, a role, an expiry and an opaque reference; its join data is evidence, never attendance.
6. **Capacity and consent are database rules**, not UI hints: a trigger on enrolments at group assignment (for any non-Rejected status, never inside a status transition such as payment-driven confirmation, but including the enrolment write that the student-form placement sync of migration 074 makes) and the guest command enforce seats; a consent row gates every child's room.

## Consequences

- One additive migration touching five vocabulary constraints, re-creating three existing functions by one line each (`crm_start_enrollment`, and the migration-096 reception objects `operational_security.valid_session_level` and `save_receptionist_enrollment`, so that reception's own commands accept the two programmes), and adding the `online_*` tables, a private `online_security` schema and narrow RPCs; new routes wired consistently in middleware, client guard, policy map and sidebar.
- The public registration form and its API keep today's eight programmes through a public subset of the shared list (plan Q15 = A, owner 2026-10-18); enrolment into an online course is reception-only, with no self-serve sign-up.
- Link secrecy and display-name immutability depend on the provider; they are acceptance tests (T-P1–T-P7 in the plan), and a provider that fails them is rejected.
- The in-person teacher portal's existing exposure of a teacher's own learners' e-mails is not fixed by this decision and stays a separate item.
- The CRM keeps its programme list, constraints, Meta mappings and cohort function (owner decision Q2 = option C, 2026-10-17): a lead is enrolled into an online group through the existing CRM path and the programme view attributes that enrolment to the lead's own programme. Because `crm_start_enrollment` hard-codes the accepted programmes, that path works only if its array gains the two online values; the owner confirmed on 2026-10-18 (plan Q2 = C1) exactly that one-line change, re-created from the function's 112 body, with a proof that nothing else in the function changed; no other CRM function, constraint, lead field, Meta mapping or funnel object changes.
- A pass is scoped to a lesson: one opaque room reference per course, and with Zoom Video SDK (plan OL-r6, decision A2) the provider session is per lesson, so a lesson-A token cannot enter lesson B at the provider; passes expire at lesson end plus grace (the provider token at that moment or 30 minutes after issue, whichever is later, A1), lessons of a course never overlap, and open passes are revoked on cancellation, guest removal and course close. ~~The activation route, the lesson-action routes, the join route and the webhook route are gated; the fake provider is refused in Production.~~ **Amended 2026-11-17** ([below](#amendment--webhook-gate-and-provider-per-pass-2026-11-17)): The activation route, the lesson-action routes and the join route are behind the live switch; the webhook route is behind its own gate (configured provider, credentials and webhook secret present and free of paste mistakes), independent of the live switch, and is closed by removing the webhook secret; the fake provider is refused in Production on every route.
- No scheduler, recording, homework, materials, WhatsApp automation, online payment or mobile app is implied.

## Alternatives considered

- **A delivery-mode flag on groups instead of new course types.** Smaller migration, but “online” would not appear as a programme on receipts, the student file or reports, against the owner's decision.
- **Tightening the 071 teacher functions to exclude online groups** while keeping `groups.teacher_id`. Predicate-by-predicate, it would have to be repeated in every policy that reads `groups.teacher_id` directly (006, 044, 059, 063/064, 069); a structural NULL is safer and testable in one assertion.
- **Shareable meeting links (Zoom or similar).** Excluded by the owner: no link to forward, provider brand hidden, join tied to the logged-in person.
- **A self-built or self-hosted video stack.** Out of proportion for a pilot of one group; the adapter keeps a later change possible.
- **Reusing the retired Premium workshop model.** Its objects are scheduled for deletion by Premium retirement Release B and it never modelled remote access.

## Amendment — whiteboard sources on the room path (2026-11-15)

**Status: implemented on a pull request (PR A of [online classroom tools](../plans/online-classroom-tools.md), OCT-r3), not merged, not deployed.** It amends decisions A6 and A7 of the OL-r6 adapter addendum for `/online/room/*` only.

- **What changes.** The room path's Content-Security-Policy gains, in `script-src` and `style-src`, exactly two path-scoped sources: `https://us04st1.zoom.us/web_client_whiteboard/` (the host the Production key gets, owner step P1 of 2026-11-15) and `https://us05st1.zoom.us/web_client_whiteboard/` (the host the development key gets, so that the pre-merge real-Zoom script runs under the exact shipped header); and `blob:` in `connect-src`, for a URL the whiteboard creates in the page and fetches. **Form shipped: the path form** (honoured by installed Chrome in the PR A runs; the exact-host fallback of D2 was not needed).
- **Why a header change.** Zoom's whiteboard program is a graph of module scripts and one stylesheet whose host and build segment are sent by Zoom's server at the join; it cannot be preloaded from `source.zoom.us` the way the media chunks are (PR #192).
- **What does not change (D2, kept from A7).** No `blob:` in `script-src`. No wildcard in `script-src`, `style-src` or `worker-src`. `worker-src` is byte-for-byte what it was (`'self' blob: https://source.zoom.us`). The catch-all policy and every header outside the room path are unchanged. Any other whiteboard host, including a move by Zoom, needs a new owner decision; the room trace names a refused host (`csp blocked directive=… host=…`).
- **Owner decisions:** D2, the P1 result and the S1 decision of 2026-11-15, in [OWNER_DECISIONS](../../ai/OWNER_DECISIONS.md#online-classroom-tools--approved-2026-11-15).
- **Data to the provider.** Whiteboard content goes to Zoom's document service while a whiteboard is open (retention UNVERIFIED, as in the plan). The room pages also exchange three fixed texts on the SDK's command channel (`eh-whiteboard:open` and `eh-whiteboard:closed` from the presenter's page, `eh-whiteboard:ask` from a participant who arrives or reconnects) so that devices without the canvas can show their read-only line (owner decisions of 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16); they carry nothing about a person. Raise hand (« Lever la main », owner decisions of 2026-11-17, client only) adds markers with the prefix `eh-hand:` on the same channel (`raise`, `lower-self`, `ask`, `raised:<seconds>`, `lower`, `lower-all`; see [SECURITY_RULES](../../ai/SECURITY_RULES.md)); they carry nothing about a person either, and nothing is stored.
- **One named, expected refusal; `'unsafe-eval'` is not allowed (owner decision of 2026-11-16).** Zoom's whiteboard program (build `mwfe40q7`, served since 2026-11-16) probes whether it may compile code from text and carries on without it; the room path refuses eval, so the browser reports directive `script-src`, blocked value `eval`, raised by a script under an approved whiteboard source (`https://us04st1.zoom.us/web_client_whiteboard/` or `https://us05st1.zoom.us/web_client_whiteboard/`). A comparison with eval allowed (diagnosis only) showed no visible or functional difference, so this refusal is accepted as it is and the header is not widened. The room trace marks it `expected=whiteboard-eval-probe`; the classroom script and the Production test fail on any other refusal, and on this one from any other source. If a later Zoom build stops working without eval, that is a new owner decision (room-path-only `'unsafe-eval'`, or wait for Zoom).
- **Evidence.** [scripts/test-online-headers.mjs](../../../scripts/test-online-headers.mjs) (the Production room policy in full, the three additions against the policy of `main`, the catch-all byte-for-byte) and the local real-Zoom report of [scripts/test-online-zoom-classroom.mjs](../../../scripts/test-online-zoom-classroom.mjs) attached to the pull request. The Production source `us04st1.zoom.us` is proven only by the owner's Production test after deployment.

## Amendment — groups and the corrected group timer (2026-11-16)

**Status: implemented on a pull request (PR B of [online classroom tools](../plans/online-classroom-tools.md), OCT-r3), not merged, not deployed.** Client only; the room path's headers are those of the amendment above.

- **Groups are on** (`subsession.enable`), for every course, with the teacher rule of D4 (always a timer, the teacher visits each group); only the host or a co-host can create or open them, decided by Zoom from the signed role. A move into a group or back is a reconnection of the same session: no new pass, no call to the join route, never a leave.
- **The room corrects the toolkit's group timer (owner decision of 2026-11-16).** The toolkit's own groups window never switches Zoom's timer on and passes a typed duration as a string, which makes the opening fail silently. The room wraps the one SDK call that opens the groups and corrects exactly two options (the duration as a number of seconds, the timer on when the box is ticked). A companion style rule keeps the toolkit's leave menu visible while a timer counts down. Both rest on undocumented toolkit behaviour, pinned by unit tests that read the installed bundle; an upgrade that repairs the toolkit removes them.
- **Data to the provider.** Group names and messages to all groups are typed by the teacher and go to Zoom; the platform adds nothing, and the room's trace prints neither (only the reason of a move and the timer's length).
- **Known limit.** On a phone the toolkit's leave window inside a group is hard-coded English and its « Leave » button leaves the whole lesson. Owner decision D12 (2026-11-17): phone learners may still be assigned to groups; the teacher tells them once to touch « To Main », never « Leave », and D11 still applies if a group fails for one of them ([OWNER_DECISIONS](../../ai/OWNER_DECISIONS.md#online-classroom-tools--approved-2026-11-15)).
- **Evidence.** [scripts/test-online-room-unit.mjs](../../../scripts/test-online-room-unit.mjs), [scripts/test-online-room-lifecycle-browser.mjs](../../../scripts/test-online-room-lifecycle-browser.mjs) and the groups half of [scripts/test-online-zoom-classroom.mjs](../../../scripts/test-online-zoom-classroom.mjs), whose report is attached to the pull request; findings in the [plan's PR B record](../plans/online-classroom-tools.md#implementation-record--pr-b-groups).
